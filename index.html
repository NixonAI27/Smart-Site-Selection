Who We Are

Luxury Built on a Foundation of Purpose

Dawson Developments is an Edmonton-based residential developer built on one belief: the most beautiful homes should also be the most responsible ones.

We design residential communities that respect the character of established neighbourhoods while bringing in the infrastructure of tomorrow. Geothermal systems, solar generation, and shared amenities that genuinely lower costs and raise quality of life.

Every project carries a name and a story. Lilly's Landing is our first, named for Lillian Dawson, whose values run through everything we build here.

The First Project

Lilly's Landing

Situated on a prominent corner lot on 98 Avenue in Forest Heights, Lilly's Landing is a collection of luxury brownstone row houses designed across four thoughtful levels, from the lane-accessed garage below grade to the rooftop terrace above.

The project takes its name from Lillian Dawson. Her legacy is a deep respect for place, for people, and for the kind of home that endures well beyond its build date.

Every level of every home has been purposefully designed. Nothing here is an afterthought.

98 Avenue · Forest Heights · Edmonton, AB

Brownstone Row Houses on the Corner Lot

Three above-grade storeys plus a fully finished below-grade level. Lane-accessed private parking, open-plan main floor living, three bedrooms above, and a spectacular rooftop patio with entertainment lounge.

Geothermal and Solar, Built In from the Start

A central geothermal loop and rooftop solar array are built into the structure, not bolted on later. Individual heat pumps serve each home, and heating costs are managed collectively through the condo association.

Green Courtyard and Lane Parking

Below-grade parking is topped with hollow-core panels and turf, giving residents a snow-protected courtyard year-round. Streets stay clean. Your vehicle stays warm.

Energy & Environment

Built to Last. Built to Lead.

Geothermal Heating & Cooling

A central geothermal loop runs beneath the development, feeding individual heat pumps in every home. The result is ultra-efficient, year-round climate control with dramatically lower emissions than conventional systems. Lower bills too, managed collectively through the condo association.

Solar Power Generation

Photovoltaic panels on the brownstone rooftops generate clean electricity managed by the condominium association. Heating costs are covered by the association; electricity cost-sharing terms are structured to benefit every resident.

Shared Green Courtyard

Below-grade parking is covered by hollow-core concrete panels and topped with turf, creating a snow-protected courtyard that residents can actually use year-round. No ice, no shovelling. Just a genuinely welcoming outdoor space in every season.

Regulatory Context

Compliant by Design,
Not by Compromise

Lilly's Landing is fully aligned with Edmonton's Small-Scale Residential (RS) Zone. Situated on a corner lot, the site comfortably supports the brownstone row-house configuration, with height, length, and entrance design all meeting current standards.

Recent zoning updates reinforce the design principles already at the heart of this project: street-facing entrances, front-facing windows, and building proportions that respect and enhance the character of a mature neighbourhood.
