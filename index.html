Who We Are

Luxury Built on a Foundation of Purpose

Dawson Developments is an Edmonton-based residential developer with a singular conviction: that the most beautiful homes should also be the most responsible ones.

We design multi-dwelling communities that honour the maturity of established neighbourhoods while advancing the infrastructure of tomorrow—geothermal systems, solar generation, and shared amenities that lower costs and raise quality of life.

The First Project

Lilly's Landing

Situated on two contiguous lots at a prominent corner on 98 Avenue in Forest Heights, Lilly's Landing is a thoughtfully layered development that brings together a scaled apartment building and a series of luxury brownstone row houses on a single, cohesive site.

The project takes its name from Lillian Dawson's home—currently occupying the non-corner lot—a nod to the heritage being honoured as the site is reimagined for the next generation of Edmonton residents.

Together, the two buildings share infrastructure, green space, and energy systems, creating a micro-community that punches far above its footprint.

98 Avenue & Adjacent Lot · Forest Heights · Edmonton, AB

Apartment Building — Non-Corner Lot

6–8 two-bedroom units, each with a master ensuite, generous patio, and central stair access. A basement mechanical unit features a forced walk-out patio; an additional north-facing basement unit completes the building.

Brownstone Row Houses — Corner Lot

Three storeys of refined living: below-grade parking from the lane, open-plan main floor, three-bedroom second floor, and a stunning third-floor entertainment level with rooftop patio and operable doors.

Shared Infrastructure & Green Space

Below-grade parking is topped with hollow-core panels and turf, creating a snow-protected courtyard. A central geothermal loop and solar array serve both buildings through individual heat pumps per unit.

Energy & Environment

Built to Last. Built to Lead.

Geothermal Heating & Cooling

A central geothermal loop runs beneath both buildings, serving individual heat pumps in every unit. The result is ultra-efficient year-round climate control with dramatically lower emissions than conventional systems—and lower bills managed through the condo association.

Solar Power Generation

Photovoltaic panels on the apartment building roof—with potential expansion to the brownstone rooftops—generate clean electricity managed by the condominium association. Heating costs are covered by the association; electricity cost-sharing terms are set to benefit all residents.

Shared Green Courtyard

Below-grade parking is covered by hollow-core concrete panels topped with turf, creating a snow-protected, year-round shared amenity space between buildings. No ice, no shovelling—just green space for residents to gather in any season.

Regulatory Context

Compliant by Design,
Not by Compromise

Lilly's Landing is fully aligned with Edmonton's Small-Scale Residential (RS) Zone, which permits up to eight units on non-corner lots of 600 m² or greater—and allows more complex configurations on corner lots.

Recent zoning updates reinforce the design principles already central to our vision: street-facing entrances, front-facing windows, and building proportions that respect and enhance mature neighbourhood character.

Edmonton RS Zone Compliant
Zone Small-Scale Residential (RS)
